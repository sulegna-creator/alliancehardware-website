About Alliance Hardware, Inc.

Established in 1997, Alliance Hardware, Inc. has become one of Cebu's trusted suppliers of quality construction materials, electrical supplies, plumbing products, industrial equipment, paints, and hardware solutions.

For more than two decades, we have proudly served contractors, engineers, architects, homeowners, and businesses by providing reliable products from trusted brands together with excellent customer service.

Guided by our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction, we continue to live by our promise:
