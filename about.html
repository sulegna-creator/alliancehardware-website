Our Story

Alliance Hardware, Inc. began its humble journey on February 7, 1997, at 72 Tomas Abella Street, Cebu City. Its roots can be traced back to the family business, LBF Hardware, Inc., which laid the foundation for the company we proudly are today.

Starting with only six dedicated employees, Alliance Hardware has steadily grown through hard work, integrity, and the trust of its customers. Today, the company is supported by more than 50 dedicated employees, continuing its commitment to delivering quality products and exceptional customer service.

In the year 2000, Alliance Hardware expanded into larger facilities and has since established 2 branches and 5 warehouses, serving customers throughout the Visayas and Mindanao with quality hardware, waterworks, safety, plumbing, and industrial products.
